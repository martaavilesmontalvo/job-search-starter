Use the results to improve your next search

Searching yourself

Keep useful openings and sources in your notes. When results miss the work, adjust a title, responsibility phrase or source. Keep firm requirements separate from preferences.

Using your AI

Continue in the chat that ran your search. Explain what helped or missed. Ask for changes, approve what fits, then search again. You do not need to return here.

Feedback you could give

“These roles focus too much on training. I want project coordination with occasional onboarding. Suggest changes before searching again.”

Switching chats or tools? Bring your latest brief, useful sources and saved openings, including applications and passes. Do not assume the new tool can read previous work.

Repeat a useful search

Use site alerts or a reminder. If your assistant supports scheduled web searches, agree on timing, time zone and delivery. Confirm that a task was created and that its first run worked. Check postings before acting; pause alerts when your search ends.
