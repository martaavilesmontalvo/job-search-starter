Start with one interview.

  1. Have the job description, résumé you sent and any interview details handy. Use the guide if you want a worked example.
  2. Start wherever you need help: understanding the role, developing an answer or organizing your notes. You don’t need to fill every space.
  3. Practice an answer aloud, then use the short notes page for the details you want to remember. Keep the full answer available for preparation.

If you want AI to help

Provide the job description, your experience notes and the interview details you have. Use this prompt to work through preparation one stage at a time.

Read and copy the interview-prep prompt
Download prompt · Text

Help me prepare for an interview. Work one stage at a time and wait for me before continuing. 1. Use or ask for the job description, résumé I sent, experience notes, interview details and notes from earlier rounds. 2. Research the company and role. Link current sources; mark unknowns. If you can't browse, ask for sources. Use interviewers' professional roles to shape my questions, without guessing what they'll ask me. 3. Suggest questions to practice and examples from my experience that could answer them. Ask what I did, why and what happened before drafting; don't invent details. Show different ways to use an example, and where another one would fit better. 4. Help me draft full answers, my introduction and why I want the role. Prepare questions that build on what I know and help me decide whether the job fits what I want. 5. Ask one practice question and wait. Ask a follow-up and wait. Tell me what was clear, what was missing and what to change. Let me retry. Include any confirmed exercise or interview format. When I ask, make short notes: my opening, why this role, questions I might be asked with example names and key details, and questions for each interviewer. Keep the full answers separately.
